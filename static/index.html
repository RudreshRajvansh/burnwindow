What this model can support

This is an empirical bridge between detection records for the selected area, grid, day/night stratum and platform. It is not a model of total fires, burned area, emissions, or pixel visibility. Evaluation years are excluded from fitting; browsing and comparing these results makes them development evidence. A future operational release needs a fresh geographic or temporal holdout and pixel QA.

The challenge and the method

NASA asks for a web application that harmonizes MODIS and VIIRS active-fire records into a calendar for historical patterns, unusual conditions, and critical periods in a selected area. BurnWindow gives global access to places and recent observations, then builds local historical comparisons from standard archives.

MODIS ≥30 confidence; VIIRS nominal/high. Standard history keeps type 0 vegetation detections. Provisional feeds lack the same type filter and are not mixed into the historical fit. Spherical equal-area cells have latitude-dependent shapes; 5/10/20 km denotes nominal area scale, not a fixed square on the ground. Time basis is UTC throughout this global engine.
